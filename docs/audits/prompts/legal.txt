## 1. Biggest Play Store issue: AccessibilityService

This is the **#1 thing I'd investigate before launch**.

Your app uses `AccessibilityService` to detect which app is currently open and enforce blocking. Google Play does allow AccessibilityService for various uses, but apps that aren't genuine accessibility tools must go through the Accessibility declaration process and provide prominent in-app disclosure and affirmative consent. ([support.google.com][1])

Your app **should not declare itself as `isAccessibilityTool=true`** merely because it uses AccessibilityService. That designation is intended for apps whose core purpose is supporting people with disabilities. ([support.google.com][2])

For ScrollGuard, you need to be prepared to explain:

> "We use AccessibilityService to identify the foreground application so that the user can enforce app-specific usage restrictions/blocking."

And your Play listing, in-app disclosure, and actual behavior need to be consistent.

Google specifically requires disclosure of what Accessibility data is accessed, how it is used/shared, and affirmative user consent for non-accessibility-tool uses. ([support.google.com][2])

### Your action

Before submission:

* Complete the Accessibility declaration in Play Console.
* Have a clear in-app disclosure before enabling Accessibility.
* Explain exactly what the service reads.
* Explain why it is required.
* Don't claim it is an accessibility tool.
* Make sure your Play Store description accurately describes the functionality.
* Prepare a short reviewer demonstration/video if requested.

# 6. Account deletion

This is an important one that I would add to your engineering checklist.

Your parent uses email/password Firebase Authentication.

Google Play's User Data policy requires apps that allow account creation to provide a way for users to request account deletion **inside the app and externally through a web resource**. Associated user data must also be deleted, subject to legitimate retention requirements that are clearly disclosed. ([support.google.com][5])

So you need to investigate:

```text
Parent creates account
        ↓
Parent uses ScrollGuard
        ↓
Parent says "Delete my account"
        ↓
Firebase Auth account deleted
        ↓
Family Firestore data deleted
        ↓
Child relationship/data cleaned up
        ↓
Any remaining associated data handled appropriately
```

Don't assume deleting the Firebase Auth user automatically deletes all Firestore data.

You need to verify the entire deletion chain.
