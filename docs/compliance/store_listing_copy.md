🔋 **Battery & Privacy Optimized**
ScrollGuard is engineered to run seamlessly in the background without draining your battery. Furthermore, your privacy is our priority: ScrollGuard uses Android’s Accessibility capabilities strictly to identify which app is open, processing everything locally on your device without ever reading your personal screen content.
