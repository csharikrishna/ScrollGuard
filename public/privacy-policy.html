ScrollGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

ScrollGuard ("the app," "we," "us") is a screen-time and focus app with two features: a personal Focus Timer that anyone can use, and an optional Parental Control mode that lets a parent supervise a paired child's device. This policy explains what information ScrollGuard collects, why, and what you can do about it. It's written to describe exactly what the app does — not generic boilerplate.

1. What ScrollGuard does

Focus Timer (no account needed): you pick apps to restrict, set a lock cycle, and the app blocks those apps on a schedule you control. This works entirely on your device — nothing about your app selections, usage, or lock cycles is sent anywhere.

Parental Control (optional, account required): a parent creates an account and pairs with a child's device using a one-time code. The parent then sets daily time allowances for specific apps on the child's device, and the child's device reports back how much time has been used so the parent can see it.

2. Information we collect

If you use only the Focus Timer

We collect nothing that leaves your device. Your app selections, timer settings, usage history, and block logs are stored only in the app's local database on your phone. We have no way to see this information, and it is not transmitted to us or anyone else.

If you use Parental Control as a parent

DataWhy we collect it
Email address and passwordTo create and secure your account (handled by Google Firebase Authentication)
The apps you choose to restrict and the time limits you setTo enforce your restrictions on the paired child device
The child device's reported usage/consumption dataSo you can see how much time has been used
A device name for the paired device (e.g. "Alex's Phone")So you can identify which device you're managing — this field is editable, so please avoid putting a child's full name in it if you'd rather not have that stored

If your device is paired as a child device

DataWhy we collect it
An anonymous device identityCreated automatically to link your device to the pairing parent's account — this is not a personal account, has no email or password, and cannot be used to sign in anywhere else
The list of apps installed on the device (names and package identifiers only)So the parent can choose which apps to restrict — we don't see what you do inside those apps
How much time is used in each restricted app, per dayTo enforce the parent's time limits and let them see usage

What we do NOT collect, from any device

  • Names, birthdates, or other identifying profile information (beyond the email address a parent chooses to sign up with)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or phonebook data
  • The content of what you type, browse, or view inside any app — ScrollGuard only ever detects which app is currently open, never what's happening inside it (see Section 4)
  • Advertising identifiers — we've explicitly turned this off; see Section 5
  • Device hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, SIM serial, etc.)

3. Camera use (QR code pairing)

ScrollGuard requests camera access only to scan the QR code shown during pairing. The camera feed is processed on your device to read the code and is never saved, recorded, or transmitted. You can also type the pairing code manually instead of using the camera at all.

4. AccessibilityService use

ScrollGuard uses Android's Accessibility API for one purpose only: detecting which app is currently open on the screen, so it knows when to show a block screen. It does not read on-screen text, take screenshots, record what you do inside other apps, or perform any actions on your behalf. Nothing this feature detects is ever sent off your device — the decision to show a block screen is made entirely locally.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics to learn about app crashes so we can fix them — this includes basic diagnostic data (device model, OS version, and what the app was doing when it crashed), but not the content of your restrictions or usage data. We do not use general analytics tracking (Firebase Analytics collection is explicitly disabled in the app), and we do not collect or transmit an advertising identifier from any device. ScrollGuard has no ads and no ad SDK.

6. Who we share information with

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. The only outside party involved is Google Firebase, which provides the authentication, database, and crash-reporting infrastructure this app runs on, acting as our service provider. No other third party has access to ScrollGuard's data.

7. How long we keep information, and how to delete it

Parental Control data is kept for as long as the pairing/account exists. When a parent unpairs from a child device, or deletes their account, we delete the family relationship, the restriction settings, the usage data, and the pairing code associated with it.

You can delete your account and its data any time — in the app, go to Parental Control → Delete My Account, or use our external deletion request page if you can't access the app. See that page for exactly what gets deleted.

The Focus Timer's local data (usage history, app selections) is stored only on your device and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it — we have no copy of it to delete on our end, because it was never sent to us.

8. Children's privacy

ScrollGuard's Parental Control feature is designed to be set up and controlled by a parent or guardian, not by the child. The child's device does not create an account, does not provide contact information, and is not asked to consent to anything — every restriction and every decision comes from the parent's device. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about what information exists about your child's paired device, or want it removed, contact us using the information in Section 10 or use the account deletion options in Section 7.

9. Security

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, protected by security rules that restrict access to only the parent and child devices belonging to a given family pairing — no other user of the app can read or write another family's data. Pairing codes are single-use, expire after five minutes, and cannot be listed or searched by anyone. We test these protections with an automated test suite covering both legitimate access and specific attempted misuse scenarios.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how ScrollGuard handles your information, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify users in the app.
