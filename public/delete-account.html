Delete Your ScrollGuard Account and Data

ScrollGuard also lets you delete your account directly in the app: open Parental Control → Delete My Account (available whether or not you're currently paired with a child device). This page is the external option, for when you can't or don't want to use the app itself.

What gets deleted

  • Your parent account (email/password sign-in) — permanently.
  • If you were paired with a child device: the family/pairing relationship, per-app restriction settings, and usage data associated with that pairing.

This does not remove ScrollGuard from the child's device or delete anything stored only locally on that device — the child (or whoever holds that device) would still need to uninstall the app there if they no longer want it installed.

How to request deletion

Email scrollguardd@gmail.com from the email address associated with your ScrollGuard parent account, with the subject line Delete my ScrollGuard account. We will process the request and confirm by reply.
